# Video Doorbells and Cloud Cameras: References

Always prefer the vendor's current support pages; app menus, feature names, and law-enforcement programs
change often. Re-verify every link and claim when updating this skill.

Last verified: 2026-10-30

## Vendor (authoritative for their own products)

- Ring, video end-to-end encryption (features unavailable while enabled, passphrase, compatible devices):
  https://ring.com/us/en/support/articles/7e3lk/Understanding-Video-End-to-End-Encryption-E2EE
  The page describes the legacy E2EE option as disabled and points to a newer E2EE; check its current
  state and feature list before advising.
- Ring, managing authorized (logged-in) client devices:
  https://ring.com/support/articles/kbzx5/Managing-Your-Authorized-Logged-In-Devices
- Ring, Control Center (two-step verification, shared users, privacy settings):
  https://support.ring.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360038142272
- Google Nest, video history in the Google Home app: https://support.google.com/googlenest/answer/10904254
- Arlo, local storage on a SmartHub or base station:
  https://www.arlo.com/en_gb/support/faq/installation-and-device-performance/local-storage
- SimpliSafe, how the indoor camera privacy shutter works:
  https://support.simplisafe.com/articles/smart-alarm-indoor-camera/how-does-the-privacy-shutter-work-for-smart-alarm-wireless-indoor-security-camera/63d937177ef83047a02af1ef
- Wyze, two-factor authentication requirement announcement (2022):
  https://forums.wyze.com/t/all-users-will-be-required-to-use-two-factor-authentication-to-log-into-a-wyze-account/240581
- Wyze, response to the March 2022 security report (first-generation camera unable to receive the fix):
  https://www.wyze.com/pages/response-to-3-29-22-security-report
- For every vendor: look for its security or vulnerability-disclosure page, law-enforcement guidelines,
  transparency report, and privacy policy, and its product end-of-life list.

## Regulators and government (factual history)

- FTC, Ring complaint and proposed order (May 2023): employee and contractor access to customer videos;
  failure to protect against credential stuffing and brute force; about 55,000 U.S. customers' accounts
  affected; multifactor authentication not adopted until 2019:
  https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2023/05/ftc-says-ring-employees-illegally-surveilled-customers-failed-stop-hackers-taking-control-users
- FTC, Ring refunds (April 2024):
  https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/04/ftc-sends-refunds-ring-customers-stemming-2023-settlement-over-charges-company-failed-block
- New York Attorney General, settlement with companies selling Eufy cameras (January 2025): streams not
  always securely encrypted and reachable via a link without authentication:
  https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2025/attorney-general-james-secures-450000-companies-selling-home-security-cameras

Generic vulnerability and incident sources (CISA KEV, NVD, CVE, CISA ransomware guidance, IC3) are listed in
the core documents.

## Third-party (not authoritative)

- The Record, Wyze February 2024 incident (about 13,000 users shown other customers' thumbnails):
  https://therecord.media/wyze-camera-security-incident-allowed-strangers-to-see-into-homes
  Relies on Wyze's statements; check Wyze's own disclosures for detail.
- PCWorld, how to opt out of Ring Community Requests (2025):
  https://www.pcworld.com/article/2945470/ring-lets-police-ask-for-security-videos-heres-how-to-opt-out.html
  Ring's law-enforcement partnerships have changed several times (a planned Flock Safety integration was
  announced in 2025 and cancelled in 2026, per Engadget:
  https://engadget.com/home/ring-calls-off-partnership-with-police-surveillance-provider-flock-safety-031717605.html).
  Check Ring's current pages before advising.
- Popular Science, how Ring's Search Party feature works and how to turn it off:
  https://www.popsci.com/diy/how-to-turn-off-ring-search-party/
- Technology Safety (NNEDV) and the Texas Council on Family Violence, Ring safety guide for survivors:
  https://www.techsafety.org/s/TCFV-Ring-Survivor_Safety_Guide.pdf
  Useful for the household-safety guidance; check app steps against the current app.
- Mozilla Foundation, Privacy Not Included reviews of consumer cameras:
  https://www.mozillafoundation.org/en/privacynotincluded/eufy-security-cams/
  Privacy-policy reviews, not security testing.
